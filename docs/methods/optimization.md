# Optimization to matched service targets

`optimization.py` (decision D010). For each product and strategy: (1) regulatory eligibility from gates (EXCLUDED strategies are not optimized; NO_CONCLUSION strategies are optimized for information and labeled); (2) full-factorial grid over the strategy's design space (`DESIGN_SPACES`) on a common-random-number batch of `search_runs`; feasible = mean fill rate >= tau and share of runs meeting tau >= q; (3) coordinate refinement (bisection toward neighbors for continuous variables, unit steps for integers) from the best feasible grid point; (4) full-N re-evaluation of the incumbent with Monte Carlo standard error. Every evaluation is stored. If no grid point is feasible the record says so and reports the closest fill rate; nothing pays a penalty to become feasible. Tests: relaxing tau/q cannot worsen the optimum; status quo is infeasible for a capacity-short product; excluded strategies produce no evaluations.
