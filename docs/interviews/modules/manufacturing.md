# Module: sterile manufacturing and process engineering

Roles: process or project engineers who have run or commissioned a small aseptic fill line, plus a CDMO commercial lead
for questions 8 and 9. Queue items this module settles: HA-21, HA-13, HA-41, and the input half of HA-30.
Length: the whole call is 45 minutes and this module has about 28 of them; the one published timetable is
`../elicitation_worksheet.md` section 2. Run the parameter cards inside the module, not before it.

**PRELIMINARY / ILLUSTRATIVE / NOT FOR EXTERNAL CLAIMS.** Every value in the "Now" column is a model input at evidence
tier 5, not a measurement. Everything in the last column is behaviour of the engine under those inputs, not a statement
about sterile-injectable manufacturing. Nothing here asserts a partnership, endorsement, customer, pilot or regulatory
opinion, because none exists. Sources cited as F-ids and L-ids are published third parties in this study's landscape
review, never a Telo relationship.

**Why the questions changed.** Fixed and resilience cost is 81.0% to 93.7% of annual cost for all eight comparators, so
every absolute cost figure in the package is a statement about the scaffold until this module lands. The study also
stopped asking whether to build. Owned distributed nodes meet the frozen service target in no region of any recorded
range on either product, and giving them the same inventory freedom that makes the alternatives feasible did not rescue
them. What is left to learn is what a line costs, what it produces, how long it takes to exist, and whether one can be
bought instead.

**How to run it.** Ask for the range before revealing the "Now" column. Ranges beat point estimates. Say which
presentation and which scale you mean before each cost answer.

## What was dropped, and why

- **Multi-product pooling costs (changeover, scheduling, validation across presentations).** The engine runs one
  presentation per network, so a pooling answer has no entry point and the threshold it would test, about five
  comparable presentations per node, is untestable here. Re-ask once that engine limit is closed.
- **Whether one quality unit can cover several registered sites.** The operational half survives as question 7. The
  legal half moved to `regulatory.md`, because it is a reading of 21 CFR 211.22(a) rather than an engineering fact.
- **Any question asking whether distributed nodes are a good idea.** They meet the target in no recorded region on
  either product and the fair-comparison correction did not rescue them. The open question about them is what they
  cost and how long they take, which questions 6, 7 and 10 ask directly.
