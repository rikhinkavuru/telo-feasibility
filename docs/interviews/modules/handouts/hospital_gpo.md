# Questions: hospital pharmacy, IDN and GPO

*This is a research feasibility study of sterile-injectable shortage response. Nothing in this conversation is
a partnership, an endorsement, a pilot, a customer relationship or a regulatory opinion, and it will not be
described as any of those. You will be cited by role and organisation type only unless you tell me in writing
that I may use more, you will see the record before anything is published, and I will follow up once at most.*

**Ranges are better answers than single numbers.** A range with a reason is the best answer of all. "I do not
know, and here is the role that does" is a useful answer. Say the units and the entity level you are answering
in; most apparent disagreement between two experts turns out to be that and nothing else.

---

| # | Question | Units and entity level | Answer form |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How many units of this presentation does your system buy in a year, and what does that number cover: which sites, which years, and does it include what you could not get? | units per year, per system | a range in units per year, with the denominator named |
| 2 | Of that volume, how much moves through a regional stocking point rather than direct, and how uneven is it across regions? | percentage, per region | a percentage split, or a statement that no stable split exists |
| 3 | At a regional stocking point, how often is the position reviewed, and to what level do you order up? | days of cover, per stocking point | a category (continuous review, periodic at a stated frequency, daily order-up-to) plus the level in days of cover |
| 4 | How many days of supply do you hold for this presentation in normal times, and where does it physically sit? | days, per echelon | a range in days per echelon |
| 5 | When an order cannot be filled, how long does it stay open before the demand is lost or substituted? Does that differ by presentation and by customer? | days, per order | a range in days, plus whether it varies |
| 6 | When this presentation is unavailable, what happens clinically, and how hard is substitution in practice? | categorical | a category (routine substitute, difficult substitute, no acceptable substitute) with the reason |
| 7 | Can you share purchase history by presentation and by site, and on what terms? | n/a | yes or no, plus format, granularity and terms |

If you also hold contracting authority, there is a separate short set of questions about price, committed
volume and standing availability. I will ask whether to run those instead, rather than adding them here.
