# Questions: GPO, wholesaler and distributor

*This is a research feasibility study of sterile-injectable shortage response. Nothing in this conversation is
a partnership, an endorsement, a pilot, a customer relationship or a regulatory opinion, and it will not be
described as any of those. You will be cited by role and organisation type only unless you tell me in writing
that I may use more, you will see the record before anything is published, and I will follow up once at most.*

**Ranges are better answers than single numbers.** A range with a reason is the best answer of all. "I do not
know, and here is the role that does" is a useful answer. Say the units and the entity level you are answering
in; most apparent disagreement between two experts turns out to be that and nothing else.

---

| # | Question | Units and entity level | Answer form |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where does inventory for this presentation sit, and how many days are held at each echelon? | days, per echelon | days per echelon |
| 2 | What are the lane times from manufacturer to distribution centre to hospital? What is the 90th-percentile transit day, and what fraction of shipments arrive more than a day late? | days, per lane; fraction, per shipment | a typical lane time in days, a 90th-percentile day, and a late fraction |
| 3 | What does warehousing and transport cost per unit for a product like this? | USD per unit, per product | a range in USD per unit, with the scope named |
| 4 | For an emergency transfer, how fast, and at what multiple of normal cost? | days; multiple of normal cost | a range in days and a multiplier |
| 5 | When supply is short, what allocation rule actually runs, who holds the right to direct product, and can that right be sold or contracted away? | categorical | a category for the rule, plus who signs, plus yes or no on whether the right is sellable |
| 6 | During an event, how far does order data drift from actual use: hoarding, allocation-capped orders, substitution masking? | fraction, per order stream | a category for the mechanisms, plus a correction range if one exists |
| 7 | What does a resilience premium look like in a contract that has actually been signed: capacity reservation, take-or-pay, failure-to-supply remedy, and what was paid? | USD per year, or USD per unit, per agreement | a category for the mechanism plus a price range, or a plain no |
